Four Training Stages

24 weeks from 2026-11-25 to 2027-05-11. Official ASC27 registration closes 2027-01-10. Prelims run 2027-01-11 to 2027-02-28 (midnight UTC+8).

4 Interactive Modules
Stage 1 · Weeks 1–3

Hardware Physics & Roofline

Watch cache lines load on row vs. column loops, slide Amdahl's serial fraction, and move kernels on a live Roofline.

 Stage 2 · Weeks 4–8

SIMD, MPI & GPU Tiling

Trigger false sharing on a 64-byte cache line, animate MPI halo swaps, and tune CUDA shared-memory tiles.

 Stage 3 · Weeks 9–13

AI Systems & GPU Porting

Pack a Transformer into GPU VRAM, animate Ring All-Reduce, watch KV cache grow, and step through AMSS-NCKU porting.

 Stage 4 · Weeks 14–24

HPL, HPCG & Power Wall

Tune HPL N/NB/P×Q grids, find the DVFS efficiency knee under 3/4/5 kW caps, and compare 10 past ASC problems.
